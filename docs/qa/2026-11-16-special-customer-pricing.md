# Customer-specific prices and discounts
Merchant owner/manager can save customer rates without modifying general catalog prices.
Cashier can request a one-use discount that another manager/owner approves in the pricing inbox.
All quotations are recalculated on the server. Sales consume bound approval IDs once.
No offline discount authorization, automatic AI discounts, or uncontrolled changes to prices.
Approval expires after 30 minutes, is item/quantity/customer/branch/cashier scoped, and is audited.
UAT: test special rates, price overrides, self-review, multiple use, expiration, roles and ledger integrity.
